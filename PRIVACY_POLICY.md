## 1. Introduction

ViveScript Solutions LLC ("we," "our," or "us") provides **TruthPop – Truth or Dare** ("TruthPop" or the "Application"). We are committed to protecting user privacy and maintaining transparency regarding data practices.

TruthPop is designed as an **offline-first party game utility**. You can play Truth or Dare immediately upon downloading the app without registering an account, providing an email, or logging into any service.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in strict data minimization:
- **No Personal Identifiers:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or government identification.
- **No Account System:** The application does not contain a sign-in or account registration system.
- **No Sensitive Permissions:** We do not request or access your device's Camera, Microphone, GPS Location, Contacts, SMS, Phone State, or Files/Storage.
- **No Audio/Photo Recording:** The game does not capture audio, video, or photos.

---

## 3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Any optional data you configure while playing—such as player names added to the temporary party turn picker, selected game intensity, language toggle, and favorited prompts—remains **100% local in device memory** during your session. This data is never transmitted to ViveScript Solutions LLC or any third-party server.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

TruthPop integrates the official **Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)** to support ongoing maintenance and development.

When your device has an active internet connection, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process certain technical data to display advertisements, including:
- **Device Information:** Device make, model, operating system version, and display configuration.
- **Advertising Identifiers:** Android Advertising ID (AAID) subject to your device settings and Google Play policies.
- **Diagnostics & Performance:** Crash logs, ad response latency, and network connection status.

For details on how Google processes and secures data, please review:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

Users can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via Android System Settings: `Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID` or `Opt out of Ads Personalization`.

## 5. Children’s Privacy

TruthPop is a general audience entertainment application. The prompts provided in the app are strictly curated:
- No explicit sexual content
- No dangerous stunts, violence, or illegal activities
- No harassment, humiliation, or non-consensual behavior
- A dedicated "Family" category suitable for all ages

We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@vivescriptsolutions.com and we will take immediate remedial action.

---

## 6. Security

Because TruthPop does not collect, host, or transmit personal user data to proprietary servers, your personal data is not stored on our systems. All gameplay logic operates locally on your device.

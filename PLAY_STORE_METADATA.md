## Full Description

Looking for the ultimate icebreaker for your next party, hangout, or cozy date night? **TruthPop** is your go-to Truth or Dare game designed to spark laughter, reveal hilarious secrets, and create unforgettable memories with just a single tap!

Whether you are chilling with close friends, bonding on a family game night, or enjoying a romantic evening with your partner, TruthPop instantly delivers engaging, creative, and safe prompts tailored to your group.

### 🎉 Why You'll Love Playing TruthPop

• **Instant Gameplay:** No setup, no accounts, and no complicated rules. Open the app and start popping questions immediately!
• **Three Fun Categories:**
  - 👥 **Friends:** Hilarious stories, friendly confessions, quirky dares, and party icebreakers.
  - ❤️ **Couples:** Sweet, romantic, playful, and heartwarming prompts to deepen your connection.
  - 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Family:** Wholesome, clean, laugh-out-loud entertainment suitable for players of all ages.
• **Three Intensity Levels:**
  - 🟢 **Chill:** Warm-up questions and easy, fun dares.
  - 🟡 **Fun:** Classic party energy with lively laughs.
  - 🔴 **Spicy:** Bold, cheeky questions and daring challenges!
• **Bilingual English & Bangla Support:** Switch seamlessly between English and Bangla (বাংলা) with one tap—perfect for South Asian party gatherings and bilingual friend circles.
• **Party Turn Picker:** Add players' names to the quick turn selector to see whose turn it is next!
• **Offline Gameplay & Private:** Play anywhere—on road trips, during flights, or at outdoor picnics. No account creation or login required. (Internet is only needed to display ads.)
• **Free With Ads:** Non-intrusive banner plus an occasional interstitial, with consent controls where required.
• **Safe & Consensual:** We believe in pressure-free fun. Every dare is optional, prompts exclude dangerous stunts or humiliating tasks, and players can skip freely with zero penalty.
• **One-Tap Sharing:** Share any funny prompt card directly to WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, or Twitter with friends who could not make it to the party!

### 💡 How to Play:
1. Choose your mode: **Truth**, **Dare**, or let fate decide with **Random**!
2. Pick your audience (Friends, Couples, or Family) and select your intensity.
3. Tap **"POP NEXT! 💥"** to reveal the prompt.
4. Answer honestly or conquer the dare—and pass the phone to the next player!

Download **TruthPop – Truth or Dare** today and make every gathering an unforgettable celebration!
